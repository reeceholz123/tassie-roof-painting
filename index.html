How It Works

How we restore your roof.

Three steps from worn-out to fully protected. Every roof, every time.

The starting point

Old tile roofs in Tassie pick up a lot of weathering. Faded glaze, moss in the valleys, hairline cracks. We climb up, document every section, and write you a fixed price before we start.

Pressure clean and prep

Strip back moss, lichen, and loose old coating with a high-pressure clean. Replace cracked or slipped tiles. Re-bed and point ridges or hips that need it. Roof comes back to a sound, dry surface ready for the system.

Two coats Dulux AcraTex

Prime, then two coats of Dulux AcraTex Roof Membrane in your chosen Colorbond colour. Self-priming, waterproof, UV-resistant. Backed by a ten year written guarantee.

Common Questions

Common questions.

Why should I get my roof painted? +
A good roof coating doesn't just freshen things up. It seals your roof against UV, rain, salt air, and the moss and lichen that thrive in Tassie's damp. It extends the life of the roof underneath, keeps the house cooler in summer, and brings the colour back. Cheaper than replacing the roof. Often makes the whole house look new.
What does roof painting cost? +
Roof painting starts from $18 per square metre. Tile roofs sit a bit higher because of the prep involved. Every roof is different though, so we climb up, measure properly, and write you a fixed price. No rough estimates. No surprises.
How soon can you start? +
Typically within a couple of weeks of you accepting the quote. Tassie weather plays a bigger part than most places. We don't paint over wet or damp surfaces, so we wait for the right window. We'll give you a firm start date before you commit, not after.
Do I need to pay a deposit? +
No deposit. Ever. You pay when the job's finished and you're happy with it. Not a day before.
Can you paint the gutters and fascia at the same time? +
Yes, and most clients have us do both at once. We'll reseal, prime, and colour match the gutters and fascia to your newly painted roof (or to your existing trim colour if you'd rather keep it). Easier to do it all in one visit than book a separate trade later.
What's included in the ten year guarantee? +
Every job is backed by a ten year written warranty on the Dulux AcraTex coating. If the paint peels, flakes, cracks, or fades in that window, we come back and sort it. You get the warranty certificate on the day we finish. Separate from that, we stand behind our workmanship too. If something's not right with how it was done, we fix it.
Can you work around solar panels? +
Yes. In most cases we carefully mask around the panels so the job gets done with them in place. If the panels need to come off for a clean finish, we'll flag it upfront in the quote and help coordinate with your solar installer.
