Roof Painting
Devonport.

Servicing Devonport · Across the NW Coast and Tasmania

Tassie Roof Painting is a roof restoration crew with over thirty years in the trade. Devonport is one of the towns we cover regularly. Roof painting, full Dulux AcraTex coatings, pressure cleaning, moss treatment, rust work, the lot. Dulux AcraTex Accredited Applicator badge to back the work.

What Devonport roofs need.

Devonport roofs sit in a particular weather mix: damp Bass Strait southerlies, salt blown in off the coast, and enough sun in summer to chalk the older Colorbond and bleach the tiles. South-facing tiles grow moss and lichen quickly. Unsealed iron rusts at the screws and along the laps. Old paint streaks and lifts.

For Devonport, we typically recommend Dulux AcraTex on Colorbond or iron, and a full clean / treat / re-coat cycle on tile. Both come with our ten year written guarantee.

What we do here.

Across Devonport and the surrounding NW Coast we handle the full painting suite. Roof painting, full coating systems, pressure cleaning and moss treatment, rust treatment, ridge work, and farm sheds. Got something that's not on the list? Give us a call, we probably do it.

How quoting works.

We come out, climb up, measure the roof, check the surface and the flashings, and write you a fixed price quote on the spot. No deposit. You pay when the job's done.
